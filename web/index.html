A Spatial-Temporal Data Storytelling Platform for Hong Kong Air Pollution

This project explores three research stories: tropical cyclones and haze, traffic-related NO2, and seasonal pollution patterns from 2020–2025.

Research scope

Scroll to move through the three stories. The visualization panel stays persistent and updates as each section enters view.

All charts display observed data only. Missing or incompatible datasets are reported explicitly.

Story 1 — Tropical Cyclones and Extreme Haze

Focus window: 24–29 July 2023 (Super Typhoon Doksuri). Hourly NO2, O3, RSP, and FSP are shown where available.

Temporal coincidence is not interpreted as causation.

Story 2 — Traffic Flow and Roadside NO2 Patterns

Compare NO2 by hour of day, weekday/weekend, and station coverage. Traffic coupling is shown only when timestamps and locations can be matched.

Story 3 — Seasonal Air Quality in Hong Kong

Daily, monthly, and seasonal summaries compare pollutants by year and season with valid-observation counts.

Story 4 — Daily Maximum AQHI Categories

How does the distribution of daily maximum AQHI categories vary across Hong Kong monitoring stations, and how frequently do higher categories occur?

The Air Quality Health Index (AQHI) is a health-risk indicator related to air pollution. These charts count how many days each month had a given daily maximum AQHI category at a monitoring station. They are counts of days, not hourly duration and not the number of people affected. Change the station to explore spatial differences.

Key finding: days in category 7 or above (including 10+)

Chart A — Categories 1–5

The upper chart shows how often each lower daily maximum category occurred in each month. Lower categories do not mean there are no health effects.

Chart B — Categories 6–10 and 10+

The lower chart shows how often higher daily maximum categories occurred. 10+ is its own category and is never treated as the number 10.

Starred readings, if present, are drawn hatched. Missing, unresolved and unknown records are not plotted as AQHI categories. South District (南區) and North District (北區) are shown as not available before June 2020, not as zero. Station coverage varies, and these descriptive counts do not explain what caused the patterns.
